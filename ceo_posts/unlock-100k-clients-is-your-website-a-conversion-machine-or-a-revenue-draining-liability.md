### The "About Us" Page That Repels Dollars

Your "About Us" page. Is it a laundry list of your team's degrees, or is it a narrative that instills confidence and showcases your deep understanding of your client's world?

Most founders talk about themselves. They list achievements. But the $100k+ client wants to know: *How will you solve *my* problem? What is your proven track record of delivering results *like mine*?*

Here’s what’s actually happening:

*   **Generic platitudes are the enemy of premium.** Phrases like "We are dedicated to client success" are white noise.
*   **Lack of social proof is a deal-breaker.** No testimonials from high-value clients? No case studies detailing multi-million dollar impacts? They’ll assume you don’t have them.
*   **Your story isn't relatable to their pain.** You’re talking about your journey; they need to see their future success reflected in your story.

I remember working with a high-end architectural firm in Nepal. Their initial website was filled with beautiful renderings of buildings, but it didn't tell the story of how they managed complex, multi-million dollar projects for discerning clients, often navigating intricate local regulations. We refocused their "About Us" and "Projects" sections to highlight their project management prowess, their client communication strategies, and the tangible ROI they delivered for luxury developments. The difference was immediate – inquiries shifted from curious looky-loos to serious potential clients.
